Draft technical-preview notice · v0.9 · 정식 서비스용 법률 문서가 아닙니다.

Data used in the preview

Account registration uses email and authentication credentials through Supabase. Account roles, game submissions, play sessions, ratings, test assignments, favorites, follows and activity records support the platform features. Administrators can access operational data as permitted by their roles.

Optional analytics records page views, play starts and sponsor events using a randomly generated browser identifier that changes daily. You may turn optional analytics off on the portal. No third-party advertising tracker is intentionally installed in this preview. Hosting and authentication providers may maintain their own technical logs. Opting out stops future optional event collection; it does not automatically erase earlier records.

AI game screening

With submission consent, game source, supplied images including the thumbnail and developer-submitted gameplay screenshots, controls, concept and AI-use disclosure, and submission metadata are sent to the AI review provider (OpenAI) to screen the game. Do not upload credentials, private keys, personal information, confidential third-party assets or files you cannot authorize for review. Static AI review is not a security certification.
