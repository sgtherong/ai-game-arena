Game submission policy / 게임 등록 정책

Draft technical-preview notice · v0.9 · 정식 서비스용 법률 문서가 아닙니다.

Submission requirements

Submit only games and assets you have rights to distribute. Disclose material third-party assets or AI-generated content where relevant to rights and licensing. Do not submit malware, credential collection, abusive content or misleading claims. HTML5 ZIP submissions are limited to small classic-script games (2 MB compressed and decompressed, up to 100 entries); external HTTPS games are unranked. Approval is manual and not proof of security. Only trusted test developers' packages should be reviewed during the preview.

Review and enforcement

Administrators may reject or pause games for safety, quality or rights issues. Never approve unfamiliar packages without independent verification. Automated malware scanning and production-grade isolation are not yet complete. Public submissions must not be opened to untrusted developers until those controls and content policies are ready.

게임 등록 기준

배포 권한을 확보한 게임만 등록하며 악성 코드, 개인정보 탈취, 타인의 저작권 침해 및 순위 조작 콘텐츠를 금지합니다. 현재는 신뢰된 테스트 개발자만 등록하도록 운영해야 합니다. 상용 배포 전 정식 검수 및 법률 검토가 필요합니다.
